During the binding phase (0–2 hours), the ternary complex (IL-6R-BS1-IL-8R, orange) saturated near 3.1 × 10⁵ complexes/cell, mirroring the IL-6R ceiling of 3.16 × 10⁵ #/cell, which confirmed IL-6R as the limiting receptor. The binary IL-8R complex (BS1-IL-8R, blue) accumulated steadily thereafter as excess antibodies engaged the more abundant IL-8R pool. The binary IL-6R complex (BS1-IL-6R, yellow) remained near zero throughout, as any IL-6R-bound antibody rapidly completed cross-linking before a stable binary species could accumulate. Upon washout at 2 hours, the binary IL-8R complex decayed while the ternary complex exhibited a brief upward inflection before plateauing — a direct manifestation of the avidity effect, wherein dissociating binary complexes release free receptors that tethered antibodies exploit to complete cross-linking. The result is sustained ternary occupancy in the complete absence of free drug, with implications for therapeutic activity beyond the pharmacokinetic window.

Computational Advancement: Extension to Glofitimab (2:1 Format)
The ODE framework was extended to Glofitimab, a 2:1 bispecific antibody with two obinutuzumab-derived CD20 arms and one CD3ε arm. This asymmetric architecture enables bivalent anchoring to tumor B-cells prior to T-cell recruitment — a mechanism absent from the 1:1 model and central to both Glofitimab's efficacy and its improved CRS profile. The model was expanded from three to five binding states: (1) Monovalent B-Cell (CD20-Ab); (2) Bivalent B-Cell Anchor (CD20-Ab-CD20); (3) Monovalent T-Cell (CD3-Ab); (4) Monovalent Synapse (CD20-Ab-CD3); and (5) Fully Locked Synapse (CD20-Ab-CD20-CD3). The Ray et al. unit conversion factor α = 8.3 × 10⁻⁷ nM/(#/cell) was applied throughout. Parameters are given in Table 2.


  



  



  



  



  



A key design choice is the asymmetric affinity: K_d,CD20 = 1.0 nM vs. K_d,CD3 = 30.0 nM. The weak CD3 affinity is intentional — it ensures T-cell engagement is restricted to contexts where the antibody is already anchored to a tumor cell, reducing systemic T-cell stimulation. This constraint is directly encoded in the model parameters and visible in the simulation output.
